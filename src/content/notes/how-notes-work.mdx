Essays are finished. Notes are not, and say so: every note carries a growth stage (seedling, budding, evergreen) and a last-tended date.

Link to anything with double brackets, like [[new-age-groceries]] or [[new-age-groceries|the groceries essay]]. The target then shows a "Mentioned in" list, and a link to a page that does not exist fails the build.
